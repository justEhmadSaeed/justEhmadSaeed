## Professional Snapshot

I work as a Senior Software Engineer at [Ignite Visibility](https://ignitevisibility.com/) and [Purpose Brands](https://www.purposebrands.com/), with experience spanning frontend architecture, backend systems, APIs, AI integrations, and cloud-ready product infrastructure.

My work sits at the center of product engineering and practical AI: shipping maintainable systems, improving reliability and speed, and turning complex requirements into software that teams can operate with confidence.

My academic foundation includes an MS in Artificial Intelligence from [LUMS](https://lums.edu.pk/) and a BS in Computer Science from [UET Lahore](https://uet.edu.pk/). I also contribute to open-source code and technical content through communities including [Open edX](https://openedx.org/), [MDN Web Docs](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/), and [React Docs](https://react.dev/).
